Biography

I am currently a Senior Researcher at JINGDONG (JD.com) and a member of its Tech Genius Team (TGT) program.

My recent research focuses on large language model training and applications, particularly efficient adaptation, data selection, and reasoning, across JD's retail ecosystem and a product corpus at tens-of-billions scale.

I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research in 2024 from the School of Mathematical Sciences, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Congying Han and Prof. Tiande Guo. Before that, I earned a B.Econ. in Financial Mathematics in 2019 from Zhengzhou University.

Research Interests

My research spans large language model training, data-centric learning, generative models, multimodal intelligence, reinforcement learning, and 3D vision. I am particularly interested in methods that connect theoretically grounded design with practical systems at scale.

  • LLM training, efficient adaptation, and data selection
  • Generative models for image, video, and 3D content
  • Machine learning and optimization
  • Large-scale retail intelligence and applications
